Connect a standard Bluetooth health device to record blood pressure, glucose, heart rate, or oxygen readings, or add a reading manually. Your profile is loaded from your configured website source. A safety rules engine and an AI agent explain what the readings may mean and what to do next.

!
Not a medical device and not continuously monitored This module reads from consumer Bluetooth devices you pair yourself. It does not connect to a pacemaker. The AI explains readings using clinician-approved wording only — it never diagnoses, never changes medication or device settings, and never says it is safe to delay urgent care. For any emergency, use your local emergency number.

Pacemaker

Authorized integration required

Not connected. A pacemaker cannot be paired from a web page or over standard Bluetooth. Its readings come from the manufacturer's monitoring platform (for example Medtronic CareLink, Abbott Merlin.net, Boston Scientific Latitude, or Biotronik Home Monitoring) or your clinic's device-check service.

To enable this section, your care provider must set up an authorized, consented data feed. Until then no pacemaker data is shown, and nothing here is inferred from your other readings.

This prototype stores readings in your browser only and does not send anything to a clinician. Bluetooth access needs a supported browser (Chrome or Edge) and a secure context. Thresholds and wording here are placeholders and must be replaced and signed off by a qualified clinician before any real use.
