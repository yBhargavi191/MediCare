1 PROBLEM STATEMENT

Non-adherence to medication is one of the most preventable causes of poor health outcomes. Patients forget doses or skip them silently, and no one — not even family or a nurse — reliably learns about it in time. Most reminder apps merely notify the patient at dose time; they cannot prove a dose was actually taken, they give no incentive to comply, and they do not escalate a miss to someone who can act.

The Gap: a system that can REMIND — VERIFY ingestion with proof — ENFORCE compliance through incentives — and ESCALATE a missed dose to a care taker instantly.

2 OUR SOLUTION

MediCare turns a scheduled reminder into an accountable, escalating event through a four-step loop:

  • REMIND — the patient gets an alert when it’s time for a dose.
  • VERIFY — a three-step AI anti-cheat proof that the dose was truly ingested.
  • ENFORCE — missing the verification window deducts a wallet penalty.
  • ESCALATE — on a missed dose, every subscribed care taker instantly receives a push notification.

3 KEY FEATURES

3.1 · AI RECOGNITION

A custom Teachable Machine model runs in-browser via TensorFlow.js and recognises the patient’s specific medicines (Tablets / syrup). Live confidence bars confirm the correct medicine is being presented.

3.2 · INGESTION PROOF

MoveNet pose AI confirms a hand-to-mouth motion (3 consecutive frames) and then checks the medicine is gone from frame — blocking “fake takes” such as merely waving the bottle near the mouth.

3.3 · COUNTDOWN

Verification must happen within a window (90 seconds in demo, 10 minutes on live doses) with a visible countdown and a manual confirm as a human fallback after an admin-configurable delay.

3.4 · WALLET PENALTY

A virtual wallet (starts at ₹500) is docked ₹10 for every missed dose, recorded as a traceable transaction.

3.5 · PUSH ALERTS

On a miss, a free serverless relay (Vercel) signs a JWT with a Firebase service account and sends an FCM web push to every subscribed care taker — even when their browser is closed. Tapping it opens a live dose-status page.

3.6 · ROLES

Patient — today’s medicines, AM/PM schedule, adherence %, wallet, alert history and care takers. Admin — aggregate dashboards and per-patient drill-down.

3.7 · ALARM

The native Capacitor wrapper fires local notifications while the app is closed, on an urgent channel that overrides Do Not Disturb and rings the device’s alarm stream.

5 HOW IT WORKS

1. Patient signs in → dashboard lists today’s medicines (AM / PM schedule).

2. Dose time arrives → alert triggers, verify page opens.

3. Verify page runs the 3-step AI proof: Show (recognise medicine) → Take (hand-to-mouth) → Gone (medicine leaves frame / swallowed).

4. Proof accepted → dose marked verified, wallet untouched, history updated.

5. Window expires without proof → ₹10 penalty, alert saved, relay → FCM → push to ALL care takers.

6. Care taker taps the notification → live dose-status page opens.

6 DEMO WALKTHROUGH

  • Log in as a patient — today’s medicines with AM/PM times visible.
  • Tap Test Reminder Now — a 90-second verification window starts.
  • Show the medicine — confidence bars confirm it is recognised.
  • Take it — hand-to-mouth motion detected; medicine confirmed gone.
  • Adherence %, verified count and wallet balance all update live.
  • Deliberately miss a dose — wait for countdown — ₹10 penalty recorded.
  • Care taker’s phone pops a push notification — tap opens live status.

7 REAL vs SIMULATED

✔ Real

Firebase Auth + Firestore, live dashboards, wallet transaction logic, the live pill classifier, MoveNet motion detection, and genuine FCM push alerts delivered through a free serverless relay.

⚠ Scoped

The ₹10 penalty is a virtual wallet (no payment gateway). Server-scheduled background reminders need the paid Blaze plan — code ready to deploy when billing is enabled. Web push is used (WhatsApp Business API documented upgrade).

8 SCALABILITY & ROADMAP

• Channel-agnostic — WhatsApp / SMS can be added beside FCM with one config change.

• Multi-medicine & multi-patient — data model already supports multiple profiles, medicines and care takers.

• Real payments — swap the virtual wallet for UPI / a payment gateway.

• Cloud scheduling — enable Firebase Blaze for server-side reminders.
